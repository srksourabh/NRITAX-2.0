Once a year

Four moves. No theatre.

01

Choose the form

ITR-2 for salary, house property, capital gains. ITR-3 if you have business or profession income.

02

Bring the papers

Form 16, 26AS, AIS, CAS if you have mutual funds. Or type. Helpers never block the path.

03

Pick the cheaper regime

Side by side, in rupees. The loser is not coloured red — owing tax is not an error.

04

Download and upload

Validate → Download JSON → portal upload. Verify within 30 days when the acknowledgement lands.
